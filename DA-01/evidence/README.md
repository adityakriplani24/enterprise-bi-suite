## Scope and limitations

These screenshots document the local report and role configuration. The SecurityMap screenshots show table-level role context; by themselves, they do not demonstrate that every report visual is correctly filtered under row-level security, nor do they establish a published Power BI Service deployment.

The screenshots are supporting project evidence and should be read alongside the main [DA-01 project documentation](../README.md).
